# Careers Academy — INDEX

The academy covering the improvable, teachable half of engineering employment:
communication, credibility, visibility, trust, networking, and job-search
execution. Companion to every technical academy here — those make you
capable; this one makes you seen, believed, and hired. Design spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-careers-academy-design.md`.

**Audience**: all engineers and technicians, junior through senior; exercises
carry `[Junior]` / `[Senior]` annotations where the framing genuinely differs.

**Content rules**: zero invented statistics (no fabricated surveys, studies,
or percentages); every work-product example complete and realistic; all
hygiene gates identical to sibling academies.
