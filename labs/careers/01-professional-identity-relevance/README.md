# Professional Identity & Relevance — Lab 01 of Careers

The careers track opens at the only place it can honestly start: the claims.
An engineer's professional identity is the set of things they can credibly say
about themselves, each one priced by two currencies — evidence (can a skeptical
reader verify it?) and relevance (does the person hiring need it?).

## What this lab actually teaches

- THEORY.md: the claim-evidence-relevance model — how identity statements
  decompose into a claim, its evidence, and its audience, and why weak
  positioning is usually a missing middle term rather than missing skill.
- CODE_DEEP_DIVE.md: three fully worked identity artifacts rewritten
  line-by-line — a summary paragraph, a skills matrix, and a positioning
  one-liner — each with the defect named before the fix.
- EXERCISES.md: five problems with worked solutions, level-annotated where
  the junior and senior framing genuinely differs.
- FLASHCARDS.md: twelve cards covering the mechanics from memory.
- COMMON_MISTAKES.md: seven identity failures with their corrections.

## The one-line summary

Not "who am I" but "what can I prove, to whom does it matter, and how
compactly can I say both".

## How to work this lab

Read THEORY.md once for the model, then treat CODE_DEEP_DIVE.md as a drill:
cover each "after" block, rebuild the artifact yourself from the "before"
and the named defect, then compare — not for wording, for what your version
priced. The exercises are the same drill against your own materials; the
flashcards close the loop the same evening. COMMON_MISTAKES is not reading,
it is the checklist you run against your live resume, profile, and intro
before this lab is done. Budget: one focused evening for the read-and-drill
cycle, a second for running the checklist on your real materials.

## Prerequisites

None — every technical academy in this repo supplies raw material, and this
lab converts it. If you arrived without any technical track yet, work this
lab with whatever shipped work you already have; the audit will tell you
where your evidence gaps are. If you have shipped nothing yet at all, the
honest output of this lab is the evidence-gap list itself — that list is
your syllabus for whichever technical academy you enter next.
