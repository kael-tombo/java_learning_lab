# VISION — Limits & Continuity
> Where this micro-lab takes you: from naive slope chasing to rigorous limits.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — limit as x→a, left vs right limits, epsilon-delta intuition.
2. **Fluency** — direct substitution, factoring-cancel, rationalizing.
3. **Discrimination** — removable vs jump vs infinite discontinuity.
4. **Scale** — IVT, EVT, asymptotic behavior at ±∞.
5. **Production** — numerics: evaluating limits stably, avoiding 0/0 in code.

## Milestones
- [ ] M1: compute lim_{x→2} (x²−4)/(x−2) by factoring.
- [ ] M2: classify a function's discontinuities from its graph.
- [ ] M3: state IVT and find an interval where a root lies.
- [ ] M4: numerical limit table approaches the analytic value.
- [ ] M5: explain why `f(1e-300)` may evaluate to 0/0 in floating point.

## Anti-Goals
- Plugging in "close to a" instead of reasoning about the limit; NaN as an answer.

## Interview Lens
- "Why does x→0⁺ differ from x→0⁻ for 1/x?" "IVT hypothesis check fails?"
