# Calculus Quiz

## Questions

**Q1.** What is lim(x→0) sin(x)/x?
- A) 0  B) 1  C) ∞  D) Undefined

**Q2.** The derivative of x⁵ is:
- A) 5x⁴  B) x⁴  C) 5x⁶  D) 4x⁵

**Q3.** ∫2x dx equals:
- A) x²  B) 2x²  C) x² + C  D) 2x² + C

**Q4.** The chain rule states d/dx[f(g(x))] =
- A) f'(g(x))  B) f(g'(x))  C) f'(g(x))·g'(x)  D) f'(x)·g'(x)

**Q5.** lim(x→∞) (1 + 1/x)^x =
- A) 1  B) e  C) 0  D) ∞

**Q6.** Which series converges?
- A) Σ1/n  B) Σ1/n²  C) Σn  D) Σ(-1)^n

**Q7.** The integral ∫[a,b] f(x)dx represents:
- A) Slope  B) Area under curve  C) Derivative  D) Limit

**Q8.** d/dx[ln(x)] =
- A) 1/x  B) x  C) e^x  D) 1/e^x

**Q9.** L'Hôpital's Rule applies to which indeterminate forms?
- A) 0/0 and ∞/∞  B) 0·∞  C) 1^∞  D) All of the above

**Q10.** The Taylor series for e^x centered at 0 is:
- A) Σx^n  B) Σx^n/n!  C) Σ(-1)^n x^n  D) Σx^(2n)

**Q11.** If f'(c) = 0, then c is called a:
- A) Inflection point  B) Critical point  C) Discontinuity  D) Asymptote

**Q12.** ∫sin(x)dx =
- A) cos(x) + C  B) -cos(x) + C  C) -sin(x) + C  D) sin(x) + C

**Q13.** The geometric series Σar^n converges when:
- A) |r| > 1  B) |r| < 1  C) r > 0  D) r < 0

**Q14.** The second derivative of position is:
- A) Velocity  B) Acceleration  C) Jerk  D) Displacement

**Q15.** ∫[0,1] x² dx =
- A) 1/2  B) 1/3  C) 1/4  D) 1

## Answer Key

1. **B** — The fundamental trigonometric limit equals 1.
2. **A** — Power rule: bring down exponent, reduce by one.
3. **C** — Indefinite integrals include the constant of integration.
4. **C** — Chain rule multiplies outer and inner derivatives.
5. **B** — This limit defines Euler's number e.
6. **B** — p-series with p=2 > 1 converges.
7. **B** — Definite integrals compute net area.
8. **A** — Derivative of natural log is reciprocal.
9. **A** — L'Hôpital directly applies to 0/0 and ∞/∞.
10. **B** — Factorial denominators characterize e^x series.
11. **B** — Zero derivative indicates a critical point.
12. **B** — Integral of sine is negative cosine.
13. **B** — Geometric series converges for |r| < 1.
14. **B** — Second derivative of position is acceleration.
15. **B** — ∫x²dx = x³/3; evaluate from 0 to 1 gives 1/3.
