# VISION — Vector Calculus
> Fields, flows, and the divergence/Stokes family.

## Arc
1. **Foundations** — vector fields, line integrals, work.
2. **Fluency** — divergence, curl, Green's theorem statements.
3. **Discrimination** — conservative vs non-conservative fields.
4. **Scale** — divergence theorem, Stokes' theorem (statement level).
5. **Production** — fluid intuition; gradient vs divergence intuition.

## Milestones
- [ ] M1: compute divergence and curl of F = (x,y,z).

- [ ] M3: check conservatism via curl==0 (in 2D: mixed partials).

## Anti-Goals
- Confusing divergence (scalar) with curl (vector); applying Stokes without orientation.
