# Calculus Exercises

## Limits (Problems 1-5)

**1.** Evaluate lim(x→3) (x² - 9)/(x - 3).
*Hint: Factor the numerator.*

**2.** Evaluate lim(x→0) sin(5x)/x.
*Hint: Use the standard limit sin(u)/u → 1.*

**3.** Evaluate lim(x→∞) (3x² + 2x - 1)/(5x² - x + 7).
*Hint: Divide numerator and denominator by x².*

**4.** Evaluate lim(x→0) (e^(2x) - 1)/x.
*Hint: Relate to the definition of e or use L'Hôpital.*

**5.** Find lim(x→1) (x³ - 1)/(x² - 1).
*Hint: Factor both as differences of powers.*

## Derivatives (Problems 6-12)

**6.** Differentiate f(x) = 3x⁴ - 2x³ + 7x - 5.

**7.** Find d/dx[x²·sin(x)].
*Hint: Product rule.*

**8.** Differentiate f(x) = ln(x² + 1).
*Hint: Chain rule with ln(u).*

**9.** Find the derivative of f(x) = e^(3x)·cos(2x).
*Hint: Product rule + chain rule.*

**10.** Find d/dx[tan(x)] using the quotient rule.

**11.** If s(t) = t³ - 6t² + 9t gives position, find velocity and acceleration at t = 2.

**12.** Find the equation of the tangent line to y = x³ at x = 1.

## Integrals (Problems 13-18)

**13.** Evaluate ∫(4x³ - 6x + 2)dx.

**14.** Compute ∫x·e^(x²)dx.
*Hint: Substitution u = x².*

**15.** Evaluate ∫[0,π] sin(x)dx.

**16.** Compute ∫(2x+1)/(x²+x)dx.
*Hint: The numerator is the derivative of the denominator.*

**17.** Evaluate ∫x·ln(x)dx.
*Hint: Integration by parts with u = ln(x).*

**18.** Find ∫[1,2] (3x² + 1/x)dx.

## Series (Problems 19-22)

**19.** Determine whether Σ(1/2)^n converges; if so, find its sum.

**20.** Test Σ1/(n² + 1) for convergence.
*Hint: Compare to the p-series with p = 2.*

**21.** Find the radius of convergence for Σx^n/n!.

**22.** Determine if Σ(-1)^n/√n converges absolutely, conditionally, or diverges.

## Applications (Problems 23-25)

**23.** Find the maximum area of a rectangle with perimeter 20.

**24.** A particle moves with velocity v(t) = 3t² - 2t. Find displacement from t=0 to t=3.

**25.** Find the volume of the solid formed by revolving y = √x from x=0 to x=4 about the x-axis.
*Hint: Disk method with radius √x.*
