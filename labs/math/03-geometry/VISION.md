# VISION — Geometry: Shape, Space & Proof Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from measuring shapes to reasoning about space.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — points/lines/angles, triangles, congruence basics, perimeter & area.
2. **Fluency** — Pythagorean theorem, circle formulas, similar triangles.
3. **Discrimination** — choose the right area/volume formula; spot degenerate inputs.
4. **Scale** — coordinate geometry, distance, vectors, transformations.
5. **Production** — geometry checks (collision, layout) with numeric robustness in mind.

## Milestones (checkable)
- [ ] M1: prove two triangles similar using AA.
- [ ] M2: compute circle segment area from radius and central angle.
- [ ] M3: derive the distance formula from Pythagoras.
- [ ] M4: detect collinearity of three points with one determinant.
- [ ] M5: implement point-in-polygon (ray casting) and test it.

## Anti-Goals
- Assuming diagrams are to scale; ignoring floating-point epsilon in "equals".

## Interview Lens
- "Area of triangle given 3 points?" — shoelace/determinant. "Overflow in squared distance?"
