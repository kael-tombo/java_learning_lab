# History: Vector Spaces

## Systems before spaces
Long before abstraction, solving simultaneous linear equations was
routine administrative mathematics. The Chinese text Nine Chapters on
the Mathematical Art devotes its fangcheng chapter to rectangular
arrays solved by column elimination, the same computation now taught
as Gaussian elimination. No exact composition date survives, so this
file gives none: the text was compiled over centuries, not published
in a single year.

## Grassmann's Ausdehnungslehre (1844)
Hermann Grassmann published Die lineale Ausdehnungslehre in 1844,
with exterior products, span-like constructions, and a notion of
dimension in near-modern generality. It sold poorly and was widely
ignored; Grassmann restated the material more accessibly in 1862.

## Hamilton, Gibbs, and vectors (1843-1901)
William Rowan Hamilton's discovery of quaternions in 1843 split
quantities into scalar and vector parts and made vector computation
respectable. Josiah Willard Gibbs' lecture notes of the 1880s dropped
the quaternion scaffolding and kept the vector part; the 1901 textbook
Vector Analysis by Edwin Bidwell Wilson, based on Gibbs' lectures,
carried that system to physicists and engineers.

## Axioms: Peano (1888)
Giuseppe Peano gave the first recognizably modern axiomatic definition
of a vector space in Calcolo Geometrico (1888), covering independence,
basis, and dimension. The definition drew little attention until
abstract algebra matured decades later.

## Infinite dimensions (1900s-1930s)
Hilbert's work on integral equations in the first decade of the 20th
century made sequence spaces central. Stefan Banach's 1920 thesis and
his 1932 book on linear operations fixed the general theory of
complete normed spaces; John von Neumann axiomatized Hilbert space
around 1929 for quantum mechanics.

## What this lab inherits
Span, independence, basis, rank-nullity: each arrived after two
millennia of elimination practice and one century of axiomatics. The
lab's rational-arithmetic checks replay the oldest layer
(elimination) inside the newest frame (axioms).
