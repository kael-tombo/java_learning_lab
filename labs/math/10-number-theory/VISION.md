# VISION — Number Theory Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from primes to patterns and proofs about integers.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — divisibility, primes, gcd/lcm, fundamental theorem of arithmetic.
2. **Fluency** — Euclidean algorithm, modular arithmetic, Fermat's little theorem.
3. **Discrimination** — when modular inverse exists; when to use CRT.
4. **Scale** — primality testing, factorization, Euler's totient, Diophantine basics.
5. **Production** — hashing, checksums, IDs, and RSA-adjacent literacy.

## Milestones (checkable)
- [ ] M1: Euclid's algorithm by hand on 1071 & 462.
- [ ] M2: find x with 3x ≡ 4 (mod 11) via inverse.
- [ ] M3: state and use Fermat's little theorem on a concrete prime.
- [ ] M4: sieve primes to 200 and count them (π(200)=46).
- [ ] M5: explain why Miller–Rabin is probabilistic and when it errs.

## Anti-Goals
- Assuming every odd >2 is prime; dividing by a in modular equations.

## Interview Lens
- "Compute a^b mod n for huge b?" — binary exponentiation. "Modular inverse existence?"
