# VISION — Algebra: Symbolic Reasoning Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from manipulating symbols to modeling with equations.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — variables, expressions, properties of equality, linear equations.
2. **Fluency** — solve linear/quadratic blind, factor, work with polynomials.
3. **Discrimination** — pick substitution vs elimination vs graphing; spot extraneous roots.
4. **Scale** — systems, inequalities, matrices preview, function composition.
5. **Production** — symbolic simplification before numeric eval; validating formulas in code.

## Milestones (checkable)
- [ ] M1: solve `3(x-2)+4=2x+7` and check by substitution.
- [ ] M2: factor `x²-5x+6`, `2x²+7x+3` unaided.
- [ ] M3: solve a 2x2 system two ways and confirm same answer.
- [ ] M4: explain extraneous roots from squaring with one example.
- [ ] M5: derive the quadratic formula from completing the square.

## Anti-Goals
- Cancelling across addition (`(x+2)/2 → x+1`); dividing by a variable that may be 0.

## Interview Lens
- "Solve in O(1)?" — linear formula. "Handle x=0 when dividing?" — case split.
