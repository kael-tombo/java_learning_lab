# VISION — Calculus Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from slopes and areas to the fundamental theorem.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — limits, continuity, derivative as slope, basic rules.
2. **Fluency** — power/product/quotient/chain rules; basic antiderivatives.
3. **Discrimination** — which rule fits; FTC part 1 vs 2; definite vs indefinite.
4. **Scale** — optimization, related rates, volumes by slicing/shells.
5. **Production** — numerical differentiation/integration awareness, error bounds.

## Milestones (checkable)
- [ ] M1: compute lim_{h→0} ((x+h)²-x²)/h from the definition.
- [ ] M2: differentiate 5 functions mixing chain/product/quotient unaided.
- [ ] M3: evaluate ∫₀¹ 3x² dx and connect to F(b)-F(a).
- [ ] M4: max/min of a cubic on a closed interval with justification.
- [ ] M5: explain why numerical differentiation is numerically unstable.

## Anti-Goals
- Differentiating without the chain rule; treating ∞ as a number in limits.

## Interview Lens
- "Area under a curve given samples?" — trapezoid. "Slope of a function at runtime?" — autodiff wins.
