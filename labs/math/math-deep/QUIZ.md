# Advanced Mathematics Quiz

## Questions

**Q1.** A group must satisfy:
- A) Closure only  B) Closure, associativity, identity, inverses  C) Commutativity  D) Distributivity

**Q2.** Lagrange's Theorem states that for finite G and subgroup H:
- A) |G| divides |H|  B) |H| divides |G|  C) |G| = |H|  D) H is normal

**Q3.** Which is a field?
- A) ℤ  B) ℤ/6ℤ  C) ℤ/7ℤ  D) 2×2 matrices

**Q4.** A metric space is complete if:
- A) It is bounded  B) Every Cauchy sequence converges  C) It is compact  D) It is connected

**Q5.** In ℝ^n, compact is equivalent to:
- A) Open and bounded  B) Closed and bounded  C) Open and unbounded  D) Closed and unbounded

**Q6.** The Heine-Borel theorem characterizes compact subsets of:
- A) Any metric space  B) ℝ^n  C) Discrete spaces  D) Function spaces

**Q7.** Uniform convergence of f_n → f means:
- A) Pointwise convergence  B) sup|f_n - f| → 0  C) f_n are continuous  D) f is bounded

**Q8.** A topological space is connected if:
- A) It is compact  B) It cannot be split into two disjoint non-empty open sets  C) It is Hausdorff  D) It has a countable basis

**Q9.** The Lebesgue integral extends the Riemann integral because:
- A) It uses partitions  B) It handles more functions (e.g., Dirichlet function)  C) It is always finite  D) It ignores measure zero sets

**Q10.** A ring differs from a field in that:
- A) Rings lack addition  B) Rings may lack multiplicative inverses  C) Rings are always finite  D) Rings are non-commutative

**Q11.** The fundamental group of a sphere is:
- A) ℤ  B) ℤ/2ℤ  C) Trivial  D) Non-abelian

**Q12.** The Riemann zeta function ζ(s) is defined for Re(s) > 1 as:
- A) Σ1/n^s  B) Σ(-1)^n/n^s  C) Π(1 - p^(-s))^(-1)  D) Both A and C

**Q13.** A homeomorphism preserves:
- A) Distances  B) Topological properties  C) Algebraic structure  D) Measure

**Q14.** The Dirichlet function (1 on ℚ, 0 elsewhere) is:
- A) Riemann integrable  B) Lebesgue integrable  C) Continuous  D) Differentiable

**Q15.** In differential geometry, the tangent space at a point on a manifold is:
- A) A scalar  B) A vector space  C) A group  D) A ring

## Answer Key

1. **B** — Four axioms: closure, associativity, identity, inverses.
2. **B** — Subgroup order divides group order.
3. **C** — ℤ/7ℤ is a field since 7 is prime.
4. **B** — Completeness = Cauchy sequences converge.
5. **B** — Heine-Borel: compact ⟺ closed and bounded in ℝ^n.
6. **B** — Heine-Borel applies to ℝ^n.
7. **B** — Uniform convergence: sup norm → 0.
8. **B** — Connected = no separation into two open sets.
9. **B** — Lebesgue handles Dirichlet function.
10. **B** — Fields require inverses for all non-zero elements.
11. **C** — Sphere's fundamental group is trivial.
12. **D** — Both series and product representations.
13. **B** — Homeomorphisms preserve topology.
14. **B** — Lebesgue integrable (integral = 0).
15. **B** — Tangent space is a vector space.
