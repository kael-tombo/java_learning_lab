# VISION — Trigonometry: Waves, Cycles & Rotation Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from triangles to oscillation and phase.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — unit circle, sine/cosine as coordinates, degrees vs radians.
2. **Fluency** — special angles, SOH-CAH-TOA, identities (Pythagorean, double-angle).
3. **Discrimination** — which identity unlocks the problem; quadrant sign discipline.
4. **Scale** — graphs of sin/cos/tan, amplitude/phase/frequency, inverse functions.
5. **Production** — sampling signals, rotating vectors, phasor arithmetic.

## Milestones (checkable)
- [ ] M1: evaluate sin/cos/tan at 0, π/6, π/4, π/3, π/2 from the unit circle.
- [ ] M2: apply `sin²θ + cos²θ = 1` and sum identities correctly.
- [ ] M3: fit `A sin(ωt + φ)` to amplitude, period, phase data.
- [ ] M4: rotate a point by θ with the rotation matrix.
- [ ] M5: explain why sampling below Nyquist aliases.

## Anti-Goals
- Confusing degrees and radians in code; assuming `asin` returns the only solution.

## Interview Lens
