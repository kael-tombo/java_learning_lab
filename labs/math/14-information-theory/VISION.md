# VISION — Information Theory Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from bits to the limits of compression and certainty.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — entropy as uncertainty, bits, Shannon's definitions.
2. **Fluency** — compute H(X), H(X|Y), mutual information I(X;Y).
3. **Discrimination** — entropy vs cross-entropy vs KL divergence.
4. **Scale** — channel capacity, source coding intuition, error-correcting codes.
5. **Production** — compression decisions, bandwidth budgets, surprise detection.

## Milestones (checkable)
- [ ] M1: entropy of a fair coin, a biased (0.9/0.1) coin, and a die.
- [ ] M2: show H(X|Y) ≤ H(X) on a small joint table.
- [ ] M3: compute I(X;Y) = H(X) + H(Y) − H(X,Y) numerically.
- [ ] M4: explain why KL(P‖Q) is not symmetric with one example.
- [ ] M5: state source-coding theorem and its consequence for gzip sizing.

## Anti-Goals
- Confusing entropy (of a distribution) with surprise of a symbol; assuming KL≥0 without proof.
