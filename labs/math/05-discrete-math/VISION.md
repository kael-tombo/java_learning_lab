# VISION — Discrete Math: Logic & Counting for Programmers Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from informal reasoning to rigorous proofs about machines.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — propositions, connectives, truth tables, predicates & quantifiers.
2. **Fluency** — set operations, relations, functions, proofs by induction.
3. **Discrimination** — pick induction vs contradiction vs contrapositive.
4. **Scale** — recurrence relations, divisibility, equivalence & partial orders.
5. **Production** — spec-driven invariants; prove the property once, test it always.

## Milestones (checkable)
- [ ] M1: truth-table `(p∧q)→p` and its converse — show they differ.
- [ ] M2: prove `1+2+…+n = n(n+1)/2` by induction.
- [ ] M3: classify a relation as reflexive/symmetric/transitive.
- [ ] M4: solve `a_n = 2a_{n-1}+1` with a₀=0.
- [ ] M5: express a loop invariant for binary search and prove termination.

## Anti-Goals
- Assuming converse of a theorem; confusing `∀∃` with `∃∀`.

## Interview Lens
- "Why does the loop terminate?" — measure decreases. "Prove meets needed?" — invariants/variants.
