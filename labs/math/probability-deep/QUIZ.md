# Probability Quiz

## Questions

**Q1.** If A and B are independent, P(A ∩ B) equals:
- A) P(A) + P(B)  B) P(A)·P(B)  C) P(A) - P(B)  D) P(A)/P(B)

**Q2.** P(A|B) is defined as:
- A) P(A ∩ B)/P(B)  B) P(B ∩ A)/P(A)  C) P(A)·P(B)  D) P(A ∪ B)/P(B)

**Q3.** For X ~ Binomial(n=10, p=0.5), E[X] equals:
- A) 5  B) 10  C) 2.5  D) 25

**Q4.** The variance of X ~ Poisson(λ) is:
- A) λ  B) λ²  C) 1/λ  D) √λ

**Q5.** The memoryless property characterizes:
- A) Normal distribution  B) Exponential distribution  C) Binomial distribution  D) Uniform distribution

**Q6.** If X ~ N(μ, σ²), then (X-μ)/σ follows:
- A) N(0, 1)  B) N(μ, 1)  C) N(0, σ²)  D) Uniform(0,1)

**Q7.** The Central Limit Theorem states that the sample mean converges to:
- A) A normal distribution  B) The population mean in probability  C) A Poisson distribution  D) Zero

**Q8.** For independent X, Y: Var(X + Y) =
- A) Var(X) + Var(Y)  B) Var(X)·Var(Y)  C) Var(X) - Var(Y)  D) √(Var(X)·Var(Y))

**Q9.** The correlation coefficient ρ satisfies:
- A) ρ ≥ 0  B) ρ ≤ 1  C) -1 ≤ ρ ≤ 1  D) 0 ≤ ρ ≤ 1

**Q10.** Bayes' Theorem computes:
- A) P(A ∪ B)  B) P(A|B) from P(B|A)  C) P(A ∩ B)  D) P(A)·P(B)

**Q11.** The MGF of X ~ Normal(μ, σ²) is:
- A) e^(μt)  B) e^(μt + σ²t²/2)  C) (1-p+pe^t)^n  D) λ/(λ-t)

**Q12.** A Markov chain is ergodic if it is:
- A) Irreducible only  B) Aperiodic only  C) Irreducible and aperiodic  D) Finite

**Q13.** The Law of Large Numbers says the sample mean converges to:
- A) The population variance  B) The population mean  C) Zero  D) The median

**Q14.** For X ~ Exponential(λ), P(X > t) equals:
- A) 1 - e^(-λt)  B) e^(-λt)  C) λe^(-λt)  D) 1/λ

**Q15.** The stationary distribution π of a Markov chain satisfies:
- A) π = Pπ  B) π = πP  C) π = P^Tπ  D) π = π + P

## Answer Key

1. **B** — Independence: P(A∩B) = P(A)P(B).
2. **A** — Conditional probability definition.
3. **A** — E[X] = np = 10·0.5 = 5.
4. **A** — Poisson variance equals mean λ.
5. **B** — Exponential is memoryless.
6. **A** — Standard normal N(0,1).
7. **A** — CLT: sample mean → normal.
8. **A** — Variance adds for independent RVs.
9. **C** — Correlation bounded in [-1, 1].
10. **B** — Bayes computes reverse conditional.
11. **B** — Normal MGF: e^(μt + σ²t²/2).
12. **C** — Ergodic = irreducible + aperiodic.
13. **B** — LLN converges to population mean.
14. **B** — Survival function e^(-λt).
15. **B** — Stationary: π = πP.
