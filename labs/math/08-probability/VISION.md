# VISION — Probability Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from "chance" to calibrated quantitative beliefs.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — sample spaces, probability axioms, equally likely outcomes.
2. **Fluency** — conditional probability, independence, Bayes' rule.
3. **Discrimination** — dependent vs independent; P(A|B) vs P(B|A).
4. **Scale** — random variables, expectation, variance, common distributions.
5. **Production** — risk scoring, A/B evaluation, uncertainty-aware alerts.

## Milestones (checkable)
- [ ] M1: compute P(A|B) and P(B|A) for a small table without Bayes memorization.
- [ ] M2: expected value of a fair die and of `2*die-3`.
- [ ] M3: variance of a Bernoulli(p) and what it implies.
- [ ] M4: simulate the Monty Hall switch strategy and confirm ~2/3.
- [ ] M5: explain base-rate neglect with one medical-test example.

## Anti-Goals
- Confusing P(A|B) with P(B|A); assuming mutually exclusive means independent.

## Interview Lens
