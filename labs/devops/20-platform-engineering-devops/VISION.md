# Vision — Platform Engineering

## Why this lab exists
Every team rebuilding the same pipeline, namespace, and dashboards is
waste. Platform engineering turns that waste into a self-service product.

## What we are building toward
- A golden path: one command from repo to running service.
- Self-service guardrails instead of review bottlenecks.
- The platform measured like a product: adoption, NPS, lead time.

## Principles
- The platform is a product; developers are its customers.
- Golden paths paved, not mandated.
- Defaults secure; escape hatches documented and tracked.

## Anti-patterns to retire
- Wiki pages that say "ask the infra team."
- Every app bespoke from the first commit.
- Platform changes shipped as surprise breakage.

## Success criteria
- A new service skeleton runs in under 10 minutes.
- Teams can self-service a preview environment.
- Platform releases come with release notes and migration guides.

## Looking ahead
This lab synthesizes everything: Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, GitOps,
Terraform, secrets, observability, and CI/CD.
