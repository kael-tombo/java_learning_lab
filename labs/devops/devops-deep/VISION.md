# Vision — DevOps Deep

## Why this track exists
Labs 01–20 teach the tools. This track teaches the judgment: how the
tools compose into systems that change safely under pressure.

## What we are building toward
- A practitioner who can design a delivery platform, not just run one.
- Someone who can reason about failure, budgeted by math.
- Someone who ships small, rolls back fast, and learns in public.

## Principles
- Everything is code, config, or a reviewed exception.
- Measure before optimizing; alert on symptoms.
- Make the safe path the easy path.
- Postmortems improve systems; blame improves nothing.

## Anti-patterns to retire
- Heroics as the release strategy.
- Manual hotfixes that never reach source control.
- Metrics without an SLO attached.

## Success criteria
- Can whiteboard a safe deploy pipeline with rollback.
- Can explain why a 5% error budget justifies a canary.
- Can run (and later lead) a blameless incident review.

## Where this leads
Staff/principal platform work: paved paths, guardrails as policy, and
reliability as a product feature.
