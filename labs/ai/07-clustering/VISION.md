# VISION — Clustering: Problem-Solving Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from k-means to density-based clustering.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — distance metrics, cluster validity, unsupervised learning.
2. **Fluency** — implement k-means, hierarchical clustering, and DBSCAN.
3. **Discrimination** — choose between centroid-based, hierarchical, and density-based methods.
4. **Scale** — Gaussian mixture models, spectral clustering, and BIRCH.
5. **Production** — choosing k, silhouette analysis, and cluster interpretation.

## Milestones (checkable)
- [ ] M1: run k-means on a 2D dataset and visualize clusters.
- [ ] M2: implement the elbow method to choose k.
- [ ] M3: compare k-means and DBSCAN on non-spherical clusters.
- [ ] M4: compute silhouette score and Calinski-Harabasz index.
- [ ] M5: explain the EM algorithm for Gaussian mixture models.

## Anti-Goals
- Assuming all clusters are spherical and equally sized.

## Interview Lens
- "How do you choose k?" "What are the limitations of k-means?"
