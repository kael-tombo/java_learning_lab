# VISION — Math for AI Deep: Problem-Solving Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from mathematical foundations to AI research.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — linear algebra, probability, calculus, and optimization.
2. **Fluency** — derive and implement key mathematical algorithms.
3. **Discrimination** — understand which mathematical tools apply to which AI problems.
4. **Scale** — advanced topics: information theory, differential geometry, and topology.
5. **Production** — mathematical reasoning for research and development.
