# VISION — Variational Autoencoders: Problem-Solving Mastery Path
> Where this lab takes you: from autoencoders to latent variable models.

## The Arc
1. **Foundations** — autoencoders, bottleneck, and reconstruction loss.
2. **Fluency** — implement VAE with reparameterization trick.
3. **Discrimination** — choose between VAE, GAN, and diffusion models for generation.
4. **Scale** — conditional VAEs, VQ-VAE, and hierarchical VAEs.
5. **Production** — latent space interpolation, disentanglement, and evaluation.
