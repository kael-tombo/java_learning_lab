# Data Wrangling - Vision
## Where this leads
- Data Wrangling practice here points toward ward-level completeness dashboards and repeat-request tracking.
- Learners finishing 01-data-wrangling repeat the schema validation, missing-value treatment, record dedup keys, tidy reshape workflow without notes.
- The Municipal service-request cleaning pipeline dossier becomes the 01-data-wrangling reference for later chapters.
- Confidence in 01-data-wrangling comes from rerun counts, not from slogans.
- Peers can audit the 01-data-wrangling trail from raw inputs to final tables.
## What changes next
- Schedule monthly re-runs of the Municipal service-request cleaning pipeline checks after 01-data-wrangling.
- Extend the 01-data-wrangling results into 02-eda for distribution checks on cleaned columns as the next chapter.
- Pair each 01-data-wrangling artifact with a one-paragraph decision memo.
- Keep 01-data-wrangling results tied to choices instead of drifting into trivia.
- Revisit the 01-data-wrangling acceptance numbers quarterly for relevance.
- Prune any Data Wrangling check that stopped informing 01-data-wrangling decisions.
## What this lab does not cover
- Data Wrangling in 01-data-wrangling does not cover statistical modeling, dashboard serving, streaming ingestion.
- Those topics need their own labs outside 01-data-wrangling.
- Claims about statistical modeling, dashboard serving, streaming ingestion do not belong in 01-data-wrangling reviews.
- Treat 01-data-wrangling completion as readiness for 02-eda for distribution checks on cleaned columns.
## Signals the 01-data-wrangling habits stuck
- The learner reaches for schema validation, missing-value treatment, record dedup keys, tidy reshape before proposing models.
- The Municipal service-request cleaning pipeline dossier gets cited by its table numbers in discussion.
- Reruns of the Civic CSV janitor reproduce counts without drama.
- New 01-data-wrangling questions get framed with grain and metric first.
- Review notes on 01-data-wrangling shrink to one page because basics hold.
## Anti-goals while studying Data Wrangling
- No benchmark chasing inside 01-data-wrangling; standard numbers suffice.
- No vendor-specific tooling claims attached to Data Wrangling results.
- No borrowed statistics presented without re-running 01-data-wrangling checks.
- No statistical modeling, dashboard serving, streaming ingestion promises smuggled into 01-data-wrangling conclusions.
