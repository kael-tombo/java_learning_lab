# Experimentation - Vision
## Where this leads
- Experimentation practice here points toward pre-registered checkout tests with guarded readouts.
- Learners finishing 07-experimentation repeat the randomization design, power calculation, t and chi-square tests, peeking guards workflow without notes.
- The Checkout A/B plan and analysis dossier becomes the 07-experimentation reference for later chapters.
- Confidence in 07-experimentation comes from rerun counts, not from slogans.
- Peers can audit the 07-experimentation trail from raw inputs to final tables.
## What changes next
- Schedule monthly re-runs of the Checkout A/B plan and analysis checks after 07-experimentation.
- Extend the 07-experimentation results into 08-bayesian-statistics for a Bayesian rewrite of the same test as the next chapter.
- Pair each 07-experimentation artifact with a one-paragraph decision memo.
- Keep 07-experimentation results tied to choices instead of drifting into trivia.
- Revisit the 07-experimentation acceptance numbers quarterly for relevance.
- Prune any Experimentation check that stopped informing 07-experimentation decisions.
## What this lab does not cover
- Experimentation in 07-experimentation does not cover multi-armed bandits, geo-experiments, long-term holdout measurement.
- Those topics need their own labs outside 07-experimentation.
- Claims about multi-armed bandits, geo-experiments, long-term holdout measurement do not belong in 07-experimentation reviews.
- Treat 07-experimentation completion as readiness for 08-bayesian-statistics for a Bayesian rewrite of the same test.
## Signals the 07-experimentation habits stuck
- The learner reaches for randomization design, power calculation, t and chi-square tests, peeking guards before proposing models.
- The Checkout A/B plan and analysis dossier gets cited by its table numbers in discussion.
- Reruns of the Power plus toy analyzer reproduce counts without drama.
- New 07-experimentation questions get framed with grain and metric first.
- Review notes on 07-experimentation shrink to one page because basics hold.
## Anti-goals while studying Experimentation
- No benchmark chasing inside 07-experimentation; standard numbers suffice.
- No vendor-specific tooling claims attached to Experimentation results.
- No borrowed statistics presented without re-running 07-experimentation checks.
- No multi-armed bandits, geo-experiments, long-term holdout measurement promises smuggled into 07-experimentation conclusions.
