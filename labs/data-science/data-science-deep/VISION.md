# Data Science Deep — Track Vision

This track is the capstone layer over analysis-deep and
ml-pipelines-deep: it assumes working familiarity with wrangling, EDA,
evaluation, and pipeline discipline, and asks for synthesis — one
portfolio-grade project executed end to end with professional hygiene.

The bar: a reviewer with no prior context can rerun everything from
raw inputs to final tables, read the evaluation card without asking
what the numbers mean, and find every limitation stated before being
asked. That means versioned data, locked test discipline, as-of
correctness where time is involved, calibrated uncertainty where
probabilities are reported, and a refresh or retrain story that does
not depend on the author's memory.

Pick the project from genuine curiosity about a question, not from
technique shopping: the techniques serve the question, the dossier
serves a skeptical reader, and the grader is reproducibility.
