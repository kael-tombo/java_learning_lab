# Model Monitoring - Mental Models

## Smoke detector
- Good detectors page rarely and only for smoke.
- A detector that beeps at toast gets its battery removed; that is alert fatigue.
- Fewer, sharper detectors beat a ceiling full of nervous ones.

## River gauges
- Reference windows are painted gauge marks on the bridge pier.
- Current readings mean something only against fixed marks, not floating ones.
- Repainting the marks is a reviewed act with a recorded reason.

## Developing photographs
- Fresh predictions are undeveloped film; labels are the darkroom.
- Judging quality before developing mistakes chemistry for failure.
- Provisional prints are fine for orientation, never for grading.

## Echo chamber
- Feedback loops are microphones placed near speakers.
- Outputs re-enter as inputs until the screech drowns the signal.
- Move the microphone: validate label sources before retraining.

## Slice microscope
- Aggregates are satellite photos; slices are microscopes.
- A healthy-looking continent can hide a sick city.
- Keep one microscope per segment that matters, focused permanently.

## Burglar alarm tuning
- An alarm that rings at every cat learns to be ignored before the burglary.
- Precision is the neighbor's trust: ring rarely, mean it every time.
- Recall is coverage: every real break-in must ring, cats excepted.

## Wine tasting flights
- Maturity turns raw predictions into finished evaluations, glass by glass.
- Tasting the barrel early tells you about youth, not about the vintage.
- Official scores wait for the bottle; provisional sips guide the cellar.

## Seismograph network
- Each slice monitor is a station; the aggregate is the national average.
- Epicenters localize to stations, never to averages: read the argmax first.
- Station outages (silent monitors) look like calm earth: check the telemetry.

## Garden weeding calendar
- References are planting dates: fixed marks the season is read against.
- Replanting (refreshing) mid-season resets every comparison without asking.
- Weed (retune) on schedule with reasons logged, not whenever the garden looks odd.
