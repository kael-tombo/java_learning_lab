# Model Monitoring - Why It Matters

Undetected drift converts directly into wrong decisions served with confidence: bad prices, bad flags, bad recommendations.

Alert precision decides whether on-call trusts the system. A handful of paging rules that are usually right beats dozens that cry constantly.

Slice tracking decides fairness and coverage: aggregates can look healthy while one cohort absorbs every error.

Mature-label discipline decides whether retraining fixes reality or chases measurement artifacts.

Telemetry presence decides whether silence means health or outage. Monitoring the monitors is not optional.

Runbook linkage decides response time. A page that names triage steps resolves in minutes; a bare symptom starts an archaeology project.

Reference discipline decides signal from noise. Pinned baselines make movement meaningful; floating ones normalize decay.

Feedback awareness decides long-term integrity. Unchecked loops turn today's predictions into tomorrow's biased ground truth.

## Detection economics

Time-to-detect prices every monitor: a drift caught in hours costs a retrain, the
same drift caught in weeks costs users plus a postmortem plus the retrain. Short
paging cadences over precomputed rollups buy that difference cheaply.

Alert precision prices on-call attention, the scarcest resource in the building: a
rule at 0.300 precision spends three wake-ups per real incident, and each false one
withdraws trust. Demoting noisy rules to dashboards is not leniency; it is budget
discipline for attention.

Slice coverage prices fairness directly: the aggregate can report green while one
cohort absorbs every error, and that pattern repeats until someone looks per slice.
Microscopes on the segments that matter convert coverage from aspiration to ledger.

## Measurement honesty

Mature-window discipline separates signal from chemistry: fresh windows undercount
late-arriving outcomes, so grading them punishes careful models and flatters hasty
ones. Equal-maturity comparison is the accounting standard, not a nicety.

Reference discipline separates movement from noise: pinned snapshots make each PSI
and KS value comparable across weeks, while floating references renegotiate the
baseline silently. Deliberate refresh with recorded reason keeps the ruler honest.

Monitor-the-monitors closes the loop: alert volume, precision, and time-to-detect
per rule turn the monitoring system itself into a measured artifact. Unmeasured
monitors rot exactly like unmeasured models.

## Integrity over time

Feedback awareness protects the training future: today's predictions become
tomorrow's labels through approvals and reviews, and unquarantined loops bake bias
into every subsequent round. Source validation before retraining is the firewall.

Incident records compound experience: each pinned case, retuned rule, and reviewed
reference makes the next detection faster. Teams that write postmortems accumulate
protection; teams that move on accumulate repeats.
