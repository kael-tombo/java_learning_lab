# Analysis Deep — Mini Project: End-to-End Dossier

Take one unfamiliar tabular dataset (1k–100k rows, open license) through
three track chapters in one sitting: wrangle it (lab 01: audit missingness,
coerce types, log every reject), chart two distributions with message
titles (lab 02), and answer one stratified comparison with counts (lab
03–04: pick the strata before computing, report group sizes alongside
rates).

Deliverable: a one-page dossier — the question, the grain, one chart,
one table with counts, and a caveats section naming what could still be
wrong (leakage risks, unmeasured confounders, small-n strata). Timebox
to a day; the constraint forces the workflow, not the modeling. Rerun
the same dossier after adding lab 05 features or lab 08-style guardrails
to feel what changes and what doesn't.
