# Descriptive Statistics - Quiz

## Question 1
When is the median preferred over the mean?
A) Always
B) With skewed distributions or outliers, where the mean is pulled away
C) For categorical data
D) For symmetric data

**Answer**: B

## Question 2
What does a high standard deviation relative to the mean suggest?
A) Tight clustering
B) High dispersion / heterogeneity
C) Normality
D) Correlation

**Answer**: B

## Question 3
Why is the interquartile range robust to outliers?
A) It uses all data points
B) It ignores the most extreme 25% on each side
C) It is always smaller
D) It is computed faster

**Answer**: B

## Question 4
What does positive skewness tell you?
A) Tail to the left
B) Tail to the right — a few large values pull the mean above the median
C) Data is symmetric
D) Data is normal

**Answer**: B

## Question 5
Why compare mean and median?
A) For cultural reasons
B) A large gap signals skewness/outliers — the mean alone can mislead
C) The median is always right
D) It proves the distribution family

**Answer**: B

## Question 6
What does a Z-score of 2 mean?
A) Two standard deviations above the mean
B) Two percent supernormal
C) Twice the median
D) The 2nd percentile

**Answer**: A

## Question 7
Why is the coefficient of variation useful?
A) It is unitless — compare dispersion across different scales/units
B) It is signed
C) It removes outliers
D) It equals the correlation

**Answer**: A

## Question 8
What does Pearson correlation measure?
A) Any dependence
B) Linear dependence only — nonlinear relations can have r ≈ 0
C) Causation
D) Frequency

**Answer**: B

## Question 9
When is Spearman preferred over Pearson?
A) Always
B) With monotonic but nonlinear relationships or ordinal data / outliers
C) For time series
D) For categorical data

**Answer**: B

## Question 10
What is the mode's weakness?
A) It is hard to compute
B) It can be unstable or unrepresentative on continuous data
C) It ignores outliers
D) It requires sorting

**Answer**: B

## Question 11
Why compute group-wise statistics instead of global?
A) Faster
B) Global aggregates can mask subgroup structure (Simpson's paradox)
C) It uses less memory
D) It is required for means

**Answer**: B

## Question 12
What does a wide confidence interval for the mean say?
A) The sample mean is wrong
B) High uncertainty about the population mean given the sample — collect more data
C) The data is biased
D) The median is larger

**Answer**: B

## Question 13
Why might variance understate risk for heavy-tailed data?
A) Variance ignores the tail; a few extreme returns dominate true risk
B) Variance is signed
C) Variance is always small
D) Variance is categorical

**Answer**: A

## Question 14
Which descriptive step should precede modeling?
A) Drop missing rows
B) Inspect distributions, missingness, duplicates, and leakage candidates
C) Fit a model
D) Standardize immediately

**Answer**: B

## Question 15
Why report counts alongside percentages?
A) Percentages without counts hide sample size — a 50% rate on 2 rows is noise
B) Counts are required by pandas
C) It looks better
D) It is needed for plots

**Answer**: A
