# Exploratory Analysis - Why It Matters

## The pooled headline risk

"Control wins 82.6 to 77.3" is true, precise, and backwards as guidance:
treatment won both wards. Any decision (funding, rollout, publication) made
on the pooled number alone acts against the evidence. Stratified reporting
is not pedantry; it is the difference between the right and the wrong
action, quantified at a 5-point swing.

## Small-group diligence scales

The n = 3 means and n = 5 correlations in this lab are toy-sized versions of
real segments (new regions, pilot cohorts, rare strata). Habits formed here
- n beside every number, fragility named, flips checked - transfer directly
to the pilot analysis that decides a launch. Segments are where companies
meet Simpson most often, because segments differ in mix by definition.

## Correlation discipline prevents waste

Chasing spend-to-visits causation (r -0.99) spends campaigns on a shadow of
age; recognizing the common driver redirects effort to what actually moves.
Every confounded correlation caught in EDA is an experiment not run for the
wrong reason.

## Records make exploration reviewable

A modelling review can only challenge what was written: which strata were
cut, which displays were seen, what would change the conclusion. Teams that
require loop-records catch mix effects and causal leaps in review; teams
that accept bare findings discover them in production, where reversals cost
reputation, not just compute.

## Decisions that hinge on strata

Rollout, funding, and publication choices made on pooled numbers act
against within-stratum evidence whenever mix differs by arm. The 5.23-point
pooled gap here would misdirect real budgets; stratified headlines redirect
correctly. EDA matters because aggregation is where evidence most often
changes sign without changing substance.

## Correlation triage as resource allocation

Each confounded r promoted to "driver" spawns experiments, campaigns, and
headcount aimed at shadows. Downgrading spend-visits to age-tracked saves
that spend for the variable that actually moves. Triage discipline is budget
discipline with statistical notation.
