# Exploratory Analysis - Mental Models

## 1. Detective, not judge

EDA gathers evidence and suspects; confirmatory analysis convicts. The
detective photographs everything (displays), interviews witnesses separately
(strata), and never mistakes a strong lead (r 0.99) for a verdict. Premature
verdicts - pooled headlines, causal claims - are how innocent variables get
blamed.

## 2. Stratify or be fooled

The default assumption: any interesting comparison hides a mix effect until
stratified. Ward composition elected the pooled winner against the
within-ward votes - an election decided by districting, not by voters. Ask
"who is in each denominator?" before "who won?".

## 3. Three signals make a finding

Bulk shape, mean/median gap, and stray pattern each whisper; together they
testify. The nine-value demo convicts skew on all three counts. Single-signal
stories (one r, one mean gap) are leads to follow, not findings to report.

## 4. Correlation is a footprint

r 0.99 says two variables walked together, not who led. Age's footprints
appear beside both spend and visits; the parsimonious story is one walker
(age) with two companions. Footprints invite tracking (design, time order),
never sentencing.

## 5. Small n, loud humility

n = 5 correlations and n = 3 group means are magnifying glasses, not
telescopes: vivid, narrow, fragile. Report them with their n attached and
their fragility named - "suggestive at n = 5" - and they inform; strip the n
and they mislead.

## 6. The record is the analysis

Undocumented exploration evaporates: why this split, what was checked, what
flipped. The written loop-record turns wandering into method and lets the
next analyst (or reviewer) retrace every step to the paradox and back.
