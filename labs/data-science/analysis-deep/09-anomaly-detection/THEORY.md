# Anomaly Detection Theory

## 1. What counts as an anomaly

A point (or short sequence) generated by a different mechanism than the
bulk: fraud among legitimate payments, a stuck sensor among good
readings. Rarity plus mechanism-shift is the definition; rarity alone
(a wealthy but legitimate customer) is just a tail, not a target.

## 2. z-score baseline

z = (x - mean) / sd. Flag |z| > 3 by convention. Strengths: one line,
scale-free, everyone understands it. Weaknesses: mean/sd themselves are
dragged by the anomalies (masking), and it assumes roughly symmetric,
unimodal bulk. Robust variants swap in median and MAD.

## 3. IQR baseline

With quartiles Q1, Q3 and IQR = Q3 - Q1, flag x outside
[Q1 - 1.5*IQR, Q3 + 1.5*IQR] (Tukey fences). Resistant to masking since
quartiles ignore extremes; still univariate and blind to combinations
that are jointly odd but marginally fine.

## 4. Isolation intuition (conceptual)

Pick a random feature and random split repeatedly: typical points hide
in crowds and need many splits to isolate, while odd points stand alone
and separate in a few. Average isolation depth over many random trees
is the anomaly score. Stated as intuition only - no theorems claimed.

## 5. Thresholds trade precision for recall

Score + threshold = detector. Lowering the threshold catches more real
anomalies (recall up) but pages more innocents (precision down). The
PrCurve demo verifies one sweep: t=0.8 gives P=0.667/R=0.500, t=0.2
gives P=0.500/R=1.000. There is no "best" threshold without costs.

## 6. Base-rate fallacy

Even an excellent detector drowns in rare events. At 0.1% prevalence
with 99% sensitivity and 1% false-positive rate, PPV is 0.0902: per
100k events, 99 true catches vs 999 false alarms - ten false pages per
real one. Verified by the BaseRate program; the arithmetic is in
MATH_FOUNDATION.md.

## 7. Operations: alert fatigue

A detector whose precision wastes on-call nights gets muted - then the
real page is missed too. Design for sustainable precision (tiers,
quiet-hours batching, auto-remediation for known signatures) and track
alert outcomes like any other metric.
