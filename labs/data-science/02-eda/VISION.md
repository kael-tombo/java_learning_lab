# Exploratory Data Analysis - Vision
## Direction
- Exploratory Data Analysis practice here points toward question-driven EDA habits that feed modeling dossiers.
- Learners finishing 02-eda repeat the univariate distributions, grouped summaries, correlation scan, missingness audit workflow without notes.
- The Rental listings EDA dossier dossier becomes the 02-eda reference for later chapters.
- Confidence in 02-eda comes from rerun counts, not from slogans.
- Peers can audit the 02-eda trail from raw inputs to final tables.
## Near-term extensions
- Schedule monthly re-runs of the Rental listings EDA dossier checks after 02-eda.
- Extend the 02-eda results into 02-visualization for chart discipline on the same listings as the next chapter.
- Pair each 02-eda artifact with a one-paragraph decision memo.
- Keep 02-eda results tied to choices instead of drifting into trivia.
- Revisit the 02-eda acceptance numbers quarterly for relevance.
- Prune any Exploratory Data Analysis check that stopped informing 02-eda decisions.
## Explicit non-coverage
- Exploratory Data Analysis in 02-eda does not cover predictive modeling, causal claims, automated reporting pipelines.
- Those topics need their own labs outside 02-eda.
- Claims about predictive modeling, causal claims, automated reporting pipelines do not belong in 02-eda reviews.
- Treat 02-eda completion as readiness for 02-visualization for chart discipline on the same listings.
## Signals the 02-eda habits stuck
- The learner reaches for univariate distributions, grouped summaries, correlation scan, missingness audit before proposing models.
- The Rental listings EDA dossier dossier gets cited by its table numbers in discussion.
- Reruns of the One-table price explorer reproduce counts without drama.
- New 02-eda questions get framed with grain and metric first.
- Review notes on 02-eda shrink to one page because basics hold.
## Anti-goals while studying Exploratory Data Analysis
- No benchmark chasing inside 02-eda; standard numbers suffice.
- No vendor-specific tooling claims attached to Exploratory Data Analysis results.
- No borrowed statistics presented without re-running 02-eda checks.
- No predictive modeling, causal claims, automated reporting pipelines promises smuggled into 02-eda conclusions.
