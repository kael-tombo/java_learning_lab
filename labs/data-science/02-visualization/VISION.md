# Visualization - Vision
## Trajectory
- Visualization practice here points toward honest chart storytelling for operations reviews.
- Learners finishing 02-visualization repeat the histograms, small multiples, slope comparisons, captioned line/bar discipline workflow without notes.
- The Transit ridership visual story dossier becomes the 02-visualization reference for later chapters.
- Confidence in 02-visualization comes from rerun counts, not from slogans.
- Peers can audit the 02-visualization trail from raw inputs to final tables.
## Follow-on practice
- Schedule monthly re-runs of the Transit ridership visual story checks after 02-visualization.
- Extend the 02-visualization results into 03-exploratory-analysis for grouped comparisons behind the charts as the next chapter.
- Pair each 02-visualization artifact with a one-paragraph decision memo.
- Keep 02-visualization results tied to choices instead of drifting into trivia.
- Revisit the 02-visualization acceptance numbers quarterly for relevance.
- Prune any Visualization check that stopped informing 02-visualization decisions.
## Out of scope here
- Visualization in 02-visualization does not cover interactive dashboard engineering, geospatial mapping, animation design.
- Those topics need their own labs outside 02-visualization.
- Claims about interactive dashboard engineering, geospatial mapping, animation design do not belong in 02-visualization reviews.
- Treat 02-visualization completion as readiness for 03-exploratory-analysis for grouped comparisons behind the charts.
## Signals the 02-visualization habits stuck
- The learner reaches for histograms, small multiples, slope comparisons, captioned line/bar discipline before proposing models.
- The Transit ridership visual story dossier gets cited by its table numbers in discussion.
- Reruns of the Six-chart transit story reproduce counts without drama.
- New 02-visualization questions get framed with grain and metric first.
- Review notes on 02-visualization shrink to one page because basics hold.
## Anti-goals while studying Visualization
- No benchmark chasing inside 02-visualization; standard numbers suffice.
- No vendor-specific tooling claims attached to Visualization results.
- No borrowed statistics presented without re-running 02-visualization checks.
- No interactive dashboard engineering, geospatial mapping, animation design promises smuggled into 02-visualization conclusions.
