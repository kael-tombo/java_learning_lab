# Quiz — Graph Coloring

15 questions. Each key gives the reason.

---

## Q1
Upper bound on greedy colouring?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Δ+1 colours.

</details>

## Q2
Strongest cases needing Δ+1?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Complete graphs and odd cycles (Brooks' exceptions).

</details>

## Q3
Bipartite iff?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

2-colourable iff no odd cycle.

</details>

## Q4
DSATUR picks next?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

the vertex with the highest saturation degree.

</details>

## Q5
χ of a tree?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

2.

</details>

## Q6
χ of K_n?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

n.

</details>

## Q7
Is 3-colourability polynomial?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No — NP-complete.

</details>

## Q8
Interval graphs: greedy optimal?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Yes, with the left-to-right order.

</details>

## Q9
Planar graphs need at most?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

4 colours (Four-Colour Theorem).

</details>

## Q10
Why is greedy order important?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A bad order can use many more than χ colours.

</details>

## Q11
DSATUR tie-break?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Largest uncoloured-degree (classic greedy degree).

</details>

## Q12
Brooks' theorem applies to?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Connected graphs; exceptions are K_{Δ+1} and odd cycles.

</details>

## Q13
Odd cycle χ?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

3.

</details>

## Q14
Even cycle χ?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

2.

</details>

## Q15
What is the saturation degree of an uncoloured vertex?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The number of distinct colours among its coloured neighbours.

</details>
