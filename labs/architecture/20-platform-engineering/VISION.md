# Vision — Platform Engineering

## The Big Picture

Platform Engineering builds internal developer platforms (IDPs) that
provide self-service infrastructure, tooling, and automation to product
teams. The goal is to reduce cognitive load on developers, standardize
operations, and accelerate delivery by treating the platform as a product.

## Why This Matters

- **Developer experience** — self-service reduces wait times and friction.
- **Standardization** — consistent tooling and practices across teams.
- **Automation** — infrastructure as code, CI/CD, and GitOps.
- **Scalability** — platform scales with the organization.
- **Governance** — security and compliance built into the platform.

## Guiding Principles

1. **Platform as a product** — treat internal platforms with product management practices.
2. **Self-service** — developers provision resources without tickets.
3. **Golden paths** — paved roads for common workflows.
4. **Automation** — eliminate manual toil through automation.
5. **Observability** — platform health and usage are measurable.

## Success Criteria

- Developers can provision infrastructure self-service.
- Standardized tooling is available to all teams.
- CI/CD pipelines are automated and reliable.
- Platform usage and health are monitored.
- Developer satisfaction with the platform is high.

## Trade-offs to Keep in Mind

| Benefit | Cost |
|---------|------|
| Developer productivity | Initial platform investment |
| Standardization | Reduced flexibility |
| Automation | Platform team overhead |
| Governance | Potential platform bottlenecks |

## The Road Ahead

Platform Engineering is the evolution of DevOps and SRE practices.
It provides the foundation for organizations to scale engineering
velocity while maintaining reliability and security.
