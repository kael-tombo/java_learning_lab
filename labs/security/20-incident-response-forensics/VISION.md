# VISION — Incident Response & Forensics: The Worst Week of Your Life
> Where this lab takes you: from a checklist nobody has read to a rehearsed capability with preserved evidence and clear decisions.

## The Arc
1. **Preparation** — playbooks, roles, communication trees, and evidence preservation readiness.
2. **Triage & classification** — severity, scope, and the first hour's decision points.
3. **Containment** — isolate, revoke, block, patch; and the difference between containment
   and eradication that people constantly confuse.
4. **Eradication & recovery** — remove the foothold, rebuild trust, verify before reopening.
5. **Forensics & learning** — timeline reconstruction, root cause, and blameless post-mortems.

## Milestones (checkable)
- [ ] M1: run a full tabletop for a realistic scenario in under 90 minutes.
- [ ] M2: write an evidence chain of custody that a reviewer would accept.
- [ ] M3: build a timeline from logs across three systems and find the contradicting gap.
- [ ] M4: distinguish a containment action from an eradication action in a real scenario.
- [ ] M5: write a post-mortem that names contributing system conditions, not just a person.

## Core Competencies
- Incident classification, severity definitions, and escalation criteria.
- Evidence handling: acquisition, hashing, chain of custody, and working-copy discipline.
- Containment trade-offs: availability versus evidence preservation versus attacker access.
- Timeline reconstruction across auth, app, and infrastructure logs; identifying gaps.

## Anti-Goals
- Destroying evidence while "cleaning up" a compromised host.
- Declaring an incident resolved without verifying the attacker is gone.
- Blameless post-mortems that quietly name an individual.

## Interview Lens
- "The box is compromised and you need to keep it running. What do you do?"
- "How do you prove you eradicated the attacker rather than just hid the symptoms?"

## 30-Day Plan
- Wk1 THEORY + EXERCISES: classify scenarios, build a timeline from sample logs.
- Wk2 QUIZ/FLASHCARDS to 90%+; write two playbooks.
- Wk3 MINI_PROJECT: a full simulated incident with evidence handling.
- Wk4 REAL_WORLD_PROJECT: tabletop exercise plus post-mortem and hardening plan.

## Done = You Can
- Run an incident end to end: classify, contain, preserve, eradicate, recover, learn —
  and produce the documentation an auditor and a post-mortem both require.
